Interaction design · FYERS · 2024–25

It's okay to take a break.

I was asked to redesign Take a Break, and that was the whole brief. I turned it into a moment that lets traders step away in peace, knowing their money is safe.

  • Illustration
  • Interaction animation
  • On-brand for FYERS

The Take a Break card inside the FYERS web Profile, Segments tab.

The feature

A voluntary pause for the rest of the day.

Some days a trader needs to stop: fatigue sets in, or the losses get heavy. Take a Break is the action they choose for themselves. One toggle in Profile, confirmed with an OTP, disables every trading segment until the end of the market session, so no new trade can be placed. Their existing holdings stay untouched, and trading resumes on the next trading day.

The brief, and what I made of it

One line of brief. One moment of reassurance.

Redesign the Take a Break section in Profile. That was the whole brief. No flows, no copy, no detail.
→

Review. Reflect. Restart.

Markets can be overwhelming, and impulsive decisions can cost you. A short break could help, step back, reassess, and return with a smarter strategy.

Take a break

The card's copy, set in type.

The idea

Two hands over the coin.

Nobody reaches for this button on a good day. A trader taking a break is tired, or has just lost money, and stepping away comes with a quiet worry: is everything I have still safe while I'm gone? So the animation answers that before any text does. Candlesticks, the noise of the market, surround a rupee coin, and two hands close over the coin to protect it. The message is simple: be at peace, your money and investments are safe with us.

I made both pieces myself: the illustration and the interaction animation. Each was drawn and animated within FYERS's brand guidelines and illustration style, so the card feels native to the product rather than bolted on.

Why it works

Care, not a restriction.

01

02

Calm, not a penalty.

03

Permission, then one action.

"Review. Reflect. Restart." sets the tone, then a single Take a break button makes the choice easy.
