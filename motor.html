UI Exploration · Fintech · 2023

A vehicle super-app, landed on a cinematic garage.

A two-week UI exercise for a motor-insurance app that wanted to be a one-stop shop, insurance, challans, PUC, documents, garages. I explored a few visual directions and landed on the immersive 3D route as the one worth taking forward.

RoleConceptualisation
Timeline~2 weeks · 2024
ToolsFigma · Blender · After Effects
ScopePure UI exercise
01, Overview

A pure UI exercise

The brief was to imagine a seamless mobile experience for a motor-insurance app that also handled challans, PUC certificates, document storage, and a vehicle vault, all in one place.

I used two weeks to explore a handful of visual languages for the home screen and land on one worth taking forward. The immersive 3D direction, above, is where I settled: your vehicle takes the stage, the utility stacks below. The rest sit here as the range that got me there.

Honest framing: this was purely a UI exercise, no user research, no shipped product, no live metrics. The value was in the range explored and the direction it produced.
02, Explorations

Three directions, one feature set

Concept 03 · Motion prototype

The closest sibling to the final direction.

A motion pass that treats the vehicle as the stage and lets the rest of the app reveal beneath it. This is the study that convinced me the immersive route in the hero was the one worth taking forward, everything below sits alongside it as the range that got me there.

Concept 02B · Illustrative

Illustration on top, sheet below.

Splits the screen into a light illustrative header and a soft-white sheet holding the modules. Warmer and friendlier than the immersive route, more personality than the utility one. Explored across two layout variants that shared the same DNA.

Concept 02A · Utility

Cards, gauges, and cashless garages.

A familiar utility layout, renewal as a gauge, challans as a card, digilocker as a stack. Scannable, closer to a banking-app mental model. Also tried in dark mode. Kept as a control point; the immersive route said more.
